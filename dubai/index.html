Questions

The honest answers.

Is this money off?
No. You place credit on your plan and we add a Self-Care Bonus on top. Nothing is discounted; there is simply more on your account than you paid in.
How does the credit work?
It sits on your account from day one and draws down as you spend. If a bill goes over what is left, you settle the difference at the till.
What if I want a deep cleanse or clinical facial?
Those are done at our Abu Dhabi salons. In Dubai your credit covers hair at both salons, and the full beauty menu plus the Signature Relaxing Facial at Al Quoz. If a clinical facial is what you are after, our Abu Dhabi voucher is the one for you.
Can I use it at both Dubai salons?
Yes for hair. Beauty is at Al Quoz, because Motor City is a hair salon.
Can I split the payment?
Season of You and All-In VIP Year split into four interest-free payments with Tabby. Dip Your Toes is paid in full.
Can I give it as a gift?
Yes. Every tier includes a gift card for a friend: you tell us who she is, and we email it to her in your name, hers to book with. Nothing is posted or printed, everything is digital. Your own credit stays on your account, because it is tied to you and the plan we build for your hair. If you want the whole voucher to be hers rather than yours, message us before you pay and we will talk it through with you first.
What if I miss this season's window?
The window reopens next quarter and the value comes back with it. You have not missed your only chance, just this season's.
